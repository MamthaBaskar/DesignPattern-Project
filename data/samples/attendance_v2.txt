# Academic Attendance and Governance Policy

Section 1: General Student Requirements
All enrolled full-time students must register their biometric presence upon entering the campus.
Minimum attendance is 80%.
Students are required to submit the form to the academic registrar before the deadline.

Section 2: Examination Clearance
Any candidate failing to satisfy the attendance threshold will be prohibited from taking final examinations.

Section 3: Appeals and Exemptions
Students with verified medical exemptions may submit an appeal within 5 business days of publication.
